KOGIEL-MOGIEL RECIPE

INGREDIENTS
  • 2 eggs
  • 10 tbsp of sugar

RECIPE

  1. Take 2 eggs and break them into a medium-sized bowl.
  2. Add 10 tablespoons of sugar to the bowl.
  3. Take a wisk or a handmixer and start whipping the mixture until thick and fluffy.
  4. Assemble the mixture into your glass of liking and serve with a spoon.
  5. Enjoy!
